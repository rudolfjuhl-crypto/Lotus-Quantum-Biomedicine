Quantum Mechanical Design of JZS-1: A Selective LOXL2 Inhibitor for Advanced 
Liver Cirrhosis Reversal and Tissue Remodeling
================================================================================
Author: Rudolf Juhl | Location: Bensheim, Germany | Date: November 7, 2026

ABSTRACT
Advanced liver cirrhosis (Fibrosis Stage F4) represents a catastrophic global 
healthcare burden with zero existing curative pharmacological options. This paper 
presents the quantum-driven molecular design of "Juhl-Zirrho-Stop-1" (JZS-1), 
a first-in-class synthetic small molecule therapeutic (C22H26N4O2S) calculated 
via a Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) architecture. Operating on the 
highly symmetric, concentric 48-qubit Lotus Core V3 processor, the simulation 
effectively stabilizes molecular orbital interactions to optimize binding 
affinities within the catalytic center of the human Lysyl-Oxidase-Like 2 (LOXL2) 
enzyme. JZS-1 demonstrates a calculated ground-state binding energy of -78.4325 
Hartree, halting pathological extracellular matrix (ECM) cross-linking and 
triggering complete endogenous enzymatic tissue remodeling.
